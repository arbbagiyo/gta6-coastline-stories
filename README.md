A story-first open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built with three.js. Explore the coastal city of Solmarina on foot and by car: steal cars, dodge traffic and pedestrians, outrun the police under a five-star wanted system, and play a five-mission story chain with original characters, dialogue, chases, a stealth tail, shootouts, and a finale escape — with a full day/night cycle, a HUD with minimap and mission tracker, synthesized sound and radio, and saves in the browser. Everything is original: the city, names, story, art, and music were made for this entry.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Keyboard and mouse: WASD move/drive, mouse camera, F enter/exit car, E interact, LMB shoot, R reload, Q radio, Esc pause.

## Hand it in

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
